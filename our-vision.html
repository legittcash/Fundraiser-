Our Vision

A community that stands together for patients in need, with fundraising that is open and responsible.

Our vision

A trusted platform where people come together to support patients in need, and where fundraising is handled in a transparent and responsible way.

What this means to us

We imagine a community where no patient has to face a serious illness with no one to hear their story, and where every donor can see that their contribution is treated with care.

To move toward this, we want every campaign to be reviewed, every beneficiary to be verified before settlement, and every donor to be able to follow the progress of the patient they chose to support.

Where we are going

We will continue to improve the platform so that it stays simple for patients and their families, reliable for donors, and open about how campaigns are run.
