FAQs

Answers to common questions about donating, adding a patient, fees, and how campaigns are reviewed.

Find the patient you want to support, open their campaign by clicking "View Details", and follow the donation instructions provided on the campaign page.

Everyone can add a patient, but a person submitting a patient must have the necessary permission or authority to provide the patient's information and photograph for fundraising purposes. All submissions are reviewed before they are published.

Simply tap the "Add a Patient" button above, then fill out the form, upload the patient's photo, and make sure the photo contains evidence that helps people understand that the patient truly needs medical treatment. Submit the form and you will receive a tracking link, which you can use to check the status.

Yes. Where the donation form provides an anonymous option, you can choose to have your name displayed as "Anonymous" on the campaign. Just tap the checkbox and your name will be hidden.

Payments are processed through our payment provider. We've integrated with Paystack, one of the trusted fintech companies. KODEP does not directly handle or store your card or bank payment details.

The payment processing fee is Paystack's standard rate, estimated here and confirmed after payment. KODEP does not directly process any payment.

We never intended to collect any fee, but the costs of hosting and storing all the informations in the database are overwhelming. The platform maintenance fee helps support the operating and maintenance costs of the fundraising platform. When the fee is enabled and applicable to a campaign, it is calculated according to the platform's stated fee policy and shown before payment is completed.

If we display the patient's account information to the public, there may be security threats. We also wouldn't be able to know how much is donated if people paid directly. Payments and settlements would also be totally out of our control, because anybody could submit fake patient photos and people might start paying directly into the account. We don't allow settlements without properly verifying who the money is going to. We can pause payment with a single click if we notice anything is wrong with a campaign.

Yes. Once we verify the beneficiary, we enable settlement, and the patient will start receiving the net amount of each donation automatically.

Approval time may vary depending on the information provided and the review process. You will receive an update through the contact information provided when submitting the campaign.

If a submission is rejected, the submitter can use the private tracking information provided after submission to check the status and view the reason for rejection where one has been provided.

Yes. After submitting a patient, the submitter receives a private tracking link that can be used to check the submission status.

No. A patient photograph is required for every new campaign submission. The submission form will not accept a campaign without one.

Only information intended for the public campaign is displayed, such as the patient's name, photograph, hospital, diagnosis, story, fundraising goal, and campaign progress. Private information such as beneficiary banking details is not displayed publicly.

For transparency, we show it so that donors can see the progress of the campaign. It lets everyone know which goal has been achieved, and it also protects our team from false claims.

The amount received by a campaign may reflect applicable payment processing fees and, where enabled and applicable, a platform maintenance fee. Any applicable charges are shown during the donation process before you complete your payment.

Yes. Donations may still be accepted after a campaign reaches its displayed fundraising goal, unless the campaign has been closed or otherwise made unavailable for donations.

No. Patient submissions are reviewed by KODEP before they become publicly visible. A submission may be approved or rejected after review.

No. Publishing a campaign does not guarantee that it will receive enough donations to reach its fundraising goal.
