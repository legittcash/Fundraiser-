KODEP Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

KODEP — Kidney and Other Disease Eradication Program
Enugu State, Nigeria

1. Introduction

KODEP ("we", "us", "our") operates a patient fundraising platform that allows individuals and authorized representatives to submit fundraising campaigns for patients who require financial assistance for medical treatment and related needs.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you use our website and services.

By using the KODEP website, submitting a patient campaign, donating to a campaign, or otherwise providing personal information to us, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

Where applicable, we will obtain the consent required by law before processing sensitive personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect the following information.

A. Patient information

A campaign submission may contain:

  • Patient name
  • Patient photograph
  • Hospital name
  • Diagnosis or medical condition
  • Medical circumstances and fundraising story
  • Fundraising goal
  • Patient contact information where provided
  • Other information voluntarily supplied in connection with the campaign

Some of this information may constitute sensitive personal data.

B. Campaign submitter information

When someone submits a campaign, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, where provided
  • Relationship to or authority to represent the patient, where provided
  • Information necessary to communicate about the campaign
  • Private tracking information associated with the submission

C. Beneficiary information

Where required for a campaign to receive donations, we may collect information necessary to verify and establish the payment beneficiary, including:

  • Beneficiary name
  • Bank name
  • Bank account information
  • Information required for payment or beneficiary verification

Beneficiary and banking information is not intended to be displayed publicly on campaign pages.

D. Donor information

When you make a donation, we may collect information such as:

  • Donor name
  • Donor email address, where provided
  • Donation amount
  • Payment reference
  • Transaction information
  • Date and time of donation
  • Information provided through the payment process

If you choose to appear as an anonymous donor, information displayed publicly will be handled according to the anonymous-donation option available on the platform.

E. Technical information

When you use our website, technical information may be processed by us or our service providers, such as information necessary to operate, secure and maintain the website.

3. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Receive and review patient fundraising submissions
  • Verify campaign information
  • Communicate with campaign submitters
  • Approve, reject, archive or manage campaigns
  • Publish approved campaigns
  • Process and record donations
  • Verify beneficiaries
  • Facilitate payment of donations to eligible beneficiaries
  • Provide campaign tracking functionality
  • Send submission, approval and rejection communications
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain and secure the platform
  • Maintain donation and campaign records
  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We will not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the purpose for which it was collected except where permitted or required by applicable law.

4. Public Campaign Information

An important feature of KODEP is that an approved fundraising campaign may become publicly visible.

Depending on the campaign, publicly displayed information may include:

  • Patient name
  • Patient photograph
  • Hospital
  • Diagnosis or medical condition
  • Fundraising story
  • Fundraising goal
  • Amount raised
  • Number of donors
  • Other campaign information intentionally included for public fundraising

Campaign submitters should understand that information published on a public campaign page may be viewed, copied or shared by members of the public.

For this reason, campaign submitters should only provide information and photographs that they are authorized to provide and that they understand may become publicly accessible if the campaign is approved.

5. Patient Authorization and Consent

A person submitting information about another patient must have the necessary authority or permission to submit that patient's information and photograph for fundraising purposes.

Where sensitive personal information is submitted, the person submitting the campaign is responsible for ensuring that the necessary consent or authorization has been obtained, subject to applicable law.

KODEP may request additional information or documentation where necessary to verify a campaign.

6. Campaign Review

Campaign submissions are reviewed before they become publicly visible.

KODEP may:

  • Approve a campaign
  • Reject a campaign
  • Request additional information
  • Suspend or archive a campaign
  • Remove a campaign where necessary

Approval of a campaign does not mean that KODEP guarantees every statement made by a campaign submitter.

7. Service Providers

We may use third-party technology and service providers to operate the platform.

These may include services used for:

  • Website hosting
  • Database and storage
  • Payment processing
  • Email delivery
  • Security and technical infrastructure

Our current infrastructure may include Vercel, Supabase, Paystack and Resend.

These providers may process information necessary to provide their respective services.

8. Payment Information

Payments are processed through payment service providers.

KODEP does not intend to collect or store your full payment-card credentials on its own systems.

Payment information may be processed directly by the applicable payment provider in accordance with its security and privacy practices.

9. Platform Maintenance Fee

Where the KODEP platform maintenance fee is enabled and the campaign is eligible for the fee, the applicable platform maintenance fee will be disclosed as part of the donation/payment information.

The platform maintenance fee is separate from payment processing charges.

Where the platform maintenance fee is not applicable, it will not be represented to the donor as a fee that will be charged.

10. Sharing of Personal Information

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary:

  • To service providers operating parts of the platform
  • To payment and beneficiary-verification providers
  • To protect the security and integrity of the platform
  • To investigate suspected fraud or abuse
  • To comply with applicable law, court orders or lawful requests
  • Where you have authorized the disclosure
  • Where otherwise permitted or required by applicable law

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also protect any private tracking links, login credentials or other information that could provide access to private campaign information.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including campaign administration, payment records, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution and legitimate operational requirements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it where appropriate and subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

13. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Request restriction of processing where applicable
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Make a complaint concerning the handling of your personal information

Some requests may be subject to legal limitations or verification requirements.
