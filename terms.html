2. Purpose of the Platform

KODEP provides an online platform through which eligible patients or authorized representatives may submit fundraising campaigns and members of the public may make donations.

KODEP provides the fundraising platform and campaign-review process. KODEP does not replace medical professionals, hospitals, doctors, financial advisers or other professional service providers.

3. Campaign Submission

Anyone submitting a campaign must provide information that is accurate and not intentionally misleading.

The submitter must have the necessary authority or permission to provide the patient's information, photograph and other materials submitted to KODEP.

The submitter must not:

  • Create a fraudulent campaign
  • Submit false medical information
  • Use another person's photograph without appropriate authorization
  • Impersonate a patient or beneficiary
  • Provide false beneficiary information
  • Use the platform for unlawful activity
  • Misuse funds or donor information

4. Campaign Review and Approval

Every campaign may be reviewed before publication.

KODEP may approve, reject, suspend, archive or remove a campaign where it considers this necessary.

A campaign being approved does not constitute a guarantee by KODEP that every statement, diagnosis, photograph, financial claim or other information supplied by the campaign submitter is accurate.

KODEP may request additional information or verification before or after publication.

5. Public Campaign Information

Once approved, campaign information may become publicly accessible.

Campaign submitters understand that public campaign information may be viewed and shared by members of the public.

Submitters should therefore avoid including unnecessary private information that is not required for the fundraising purpose.

6. Donations

Donations are voluntary contributions made through the available payment system.

KODEP does not guarantee that a campaign will reach its fundraising goal.

The amount displayed as raised may be affected by applicable payment processing charges, platform maintenance fees and other applicable adjustments.

A campaign reaching its goal does not necessarily mean that additional donations will be automatically prevented unless the platform specifically implements such a restriction.

7. Payment Processing Fee

Payment processing charges may apply to donations.

These charges are determined by the applicable payment provider and may change according to the provider's terms.

KODEP is not responsible for changes made by the payment provider to its fees or payment policies.

8. Platform Maintenance Fee

KODEP may apply a platform maintenance fee to eligible donations.

The current platform configuration may apply a fee of 1% of a transaction, subject to a maximum of ₦1,000 per transaction, when the platform maintenance fee is enabled and the relevant campaign is eligible.

The applicable fee will be disclosed to the donor before payment.

The platform maintenance fee may be switched off by KODEP.

Where the fee is not applicable, it will not be presented as a charge to the donor.

9. Campaign Amount Received

The amount ultimately received by a campaign may be less than the gross donation amount because of applicable payment processing charges and, where applicable, the KODEP platform maintenance fee.

Where applicable, the platform will show the donor a breakdown of:

Donation amount
Payment processing fee
Platform maintenance fee
Campaign received

10. Beneficiary Verification

Where a campaign requires beneficiary verification or payment-account setup, KODEP may require information necessary to verify the beneficiary and facilitate settlement.

KODEP may prevent donations from being settled to a beneficiary until required verification or settlement requirements have been completed.

Beneficiary and banking information should not be publicly displayed on campaign pages.

11. Donor Information

Donors must provide truthful information when required by the payment or donation process.

Donors must not use the platform to:

  • Conduct fraudulent transactions
  • Use stolen payment credentials
  • Launder money
  • Abuse the donation system
  • Harass campaign beneficiaries or submitters
  • Attempt unauthorized access to the platform

12. Anonymous Donations

Where the platform provides an anonymous donation option, the public campaign page may display the donation as anonymous.

Information required for payment processing, fraud prevention, legal compliance or internal records may still be retained where applicable.

13. Campaign Tracking

KODEP may provide campaign submitters with private tracking links that allow them to monitor the status of their submissions.

A tracking link should be treated as private.

Do not intentionally share a private tracking link with unauthorized persons if it contains information intended only for the campaign submitter.

Donors should make their own decisions about whether and how much to donate.
